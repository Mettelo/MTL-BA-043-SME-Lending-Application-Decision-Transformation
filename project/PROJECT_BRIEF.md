# Project brief

## Appointment

You are the Business Analyst assigned to Alderbridge Business Finance Ltd. SME applications arrive through email, forms and relationship managers. Repeated document requests, inconsistent status labels and missing decision rationales create delay. The sponsor proposes automated credit decisions.

The sponsor's preferred purchase is a hypothesis. Establish the business need, investigate conflicting accounts and make a defensible recommendation. Budget: £150,000 one-off and £30,000 annual operating cost; eight-week analysis engagement, followed by a separately authorised pilot. No production implementation is authorised by this exercise.

## Boundary

Application receipt through document checks, underwriting, approval and decision communication for the stated limited-company product. Include exceptions, audit, human oversight and data handling. Exclude pricing-model development, live lending decisions and collections.

## Expected participant work

Produce a problem/scope statement, elicitation plan and stakeholder analysis; current-state and future-state models; evidence-based root-cause analysis; requirements and business rules; prioritisation and traceability; options appraisal with costs/assumptions; acceptance criteria and UAT approach; change impacts, transition controls and benefits evaluation. Choose useful notation rather than completing templates mechanically.

## Success

Sponsor aspiration: reduce repeat document requests and elapsed decision time while preserving accountable underwriting and audit evidence. Proposed goal: 80% of complete applications decided within five working days; incomplete/withdrawn applications still need visible reporting.

Agree definitions, denominators, baseline period, exclusions and ownership before treating targets as approved. Quantify uncertainty and distinguish evidence from stakeholder opinion.

## Decisions

Sponsor approves scope/funding. Operational owners validate working practices. Relevant control owners approve policy and safety/security interpretations. The BA facilitates and records decisions; the BA cannot waive controls.
