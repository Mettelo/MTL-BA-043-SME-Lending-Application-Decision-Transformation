# Scope and constraints

Application receipt through document checks, underwriting, approval and decision communication for the stated limited-company product. Include exceptions, audit, human oversight and data handling. Exclude pricing-model development, live lending decisions and collections.

No automatic approval is authorised. Credit Risk controls policy; Compliance must confirm regulatory perimeter and applicable obligations before any product expansion. Bureau access is by existing contract; no new data source is approved.

No production access is provided. Staff can attend one 45-minute workshop each week. Finance needs one-off and recurring costs separately, with assumptions about effort and adoption. Any change to policy requires the named policy owner; funding approval alone does not approve a control exception.
