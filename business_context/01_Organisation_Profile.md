# Organisation profile

Alderbridge Business Finance Ltd is fictional. A fictional UK commercial lender with eight relationship managers, six underwriters and a small operations team. This pilot concerns unsecured £30,000–£150,000 business loans to UK limited companies only. Sole traders and partnerships are outside this pilot. June dashboard counts 180 applications; supplied cases are a purposive sample.

The sponsor receives a monthly dashboard but operational teams maintain separate records. The reporting period for supplied case samples is June 2026 unless a source states otherwise. The sample is deliberately small and is not a representative population. External statistics are contextual and must never be combined with synthetic case records as though they describe this organisation.
