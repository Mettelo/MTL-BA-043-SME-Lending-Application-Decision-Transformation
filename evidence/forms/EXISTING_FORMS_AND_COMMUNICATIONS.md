## Application cover form

Accounts attached: yes / no  
Bank evidence attached: yes / no  
Relationship manager comment: ____

The form does not distinguish document presence from verification, indicative discussion from final decision, applicable policy/version, source-document period or the person authorising an exception. It is source evidence, not a future form specification.

## Document request macro
“We need your accounts and bank information before we can proceed. Please send the missing documents.”
No specific missing period, version, already-received item or next owner is required. The template does not prove every repeated request was unnecessary.

## Indicative response
“Your application is approved in principle, subject to checks. Your manager will be in touch.”
Relationship managers consider this useful commercial language; the customer account describes uncertainty about what is reliable. No approved replacement wording or promise of funding is supplied.

## Underwriter worksheet
Application ID: ____ / complete: yes / no  
Risk comment: ____ / policy exception: yes / no  
Decision: ____ / manager email: ____  
Evidence reference: ____ / date: ____  

The worksheet has no controlled policy-version field or complete named-person approval trail. A copied email is sometimes retained separately. The source does not establish that all decisions lack evidence or that this worksheet is authoritative.

## Withdrawal note and reminder
Local note: “Customer no longer proceeding. Close when checked.”
Reminder macro: “Your application is waiting for information. Please upload the outstanding documents.”

The relationship between closure, withdrawal, reminders and evidence retention is not consistently specified. A0556's source withdrawal date makes this a relevant investigation; no exact reminder-delivery history is supplied.

## Use of these sources
Investigate ownership, meaning, evidence, exceptions and communication. Do not enter real company/personal/bank data. Preserve raw forms and create any proposed redesign, field/rule catalogue, user stories, acceptance criteria and traceability in your separate delivery repository. The source pack does not supply those BA outputs.
