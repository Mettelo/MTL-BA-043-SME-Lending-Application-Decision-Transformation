# Workflow experiment and decision-readiness evidence
**Mettelo BA-043 · Stage 6 · Compiled/released 24 August**
Isolated synthetic replay; no real credit approval, data transfer, funding or production go-live.

## Setup and limitations
Forty historical cases A0551–A0590 were replayed in a baseline view on 17 August and a supplier workflow on 19 August, producing 80 paired-task observations. Two experienced Operations staff and two underwriters volunteered. Order was fixed, cases became familiar and assistance was available. This is not 80 independent users, new applications or a credit-performance validation.

The prototype loads fabricated documents/references and uses the existing workbook's recorded classifications. No real bureau data or Ledger write-back is connected. ## Dataset meaning
paired_case_id joins baseline/workflow observations for the same source_application_id. task_seconds measures an operator task, not customer elapsed time. policy_version_visible, rationale_retained and human_review_evidenced are observer flags, not regulatory or credit-quality certification. operator_assisted affects independence.

The supplier calls a shorter task time support for its 70% automation claim. No proportion of safe automated decisions, actual funding outcome, customer understanding, arrears or model accuracy was measured. The quote exclusions and Compliance objection remain.

## Separate weekly follow-up
The work group began 10 August with 12 pending applications. During 10–16 August it received 30, decided 26 and recorded one withdrawal, ending with 15. During 17–23 August it received 34, decided 27 and recorded two withdrawals, ending with 20. The flows reconcile: 12+30−26−1=15; 15+34−27−2=20. They are a new synthetic aggregate, not the June cohort.

Document-request events fell from 20 to 15, while supported underwriting/Operations hours rose from 61 to 78. Cases and support differed, and the workflow remained isolated. These figures do not prove causal benefit, risk improvement or a sustainable cost saving.
