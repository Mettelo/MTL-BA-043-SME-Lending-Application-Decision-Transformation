# Extraction notes

Cases: ten synthetic limited-company applications received in June; snapshot 30 June at 18:00 UK. A case is not a director or a loan drawdown. One row per application; no real borrower data. Blank dates mean not observed. Amount is requested GBP, not approved or funded amount. outcome pending uses approver_role 'pending' to mean no decision maker recorded, not an authority role.
Rework: one row per document request; repeated requests for A003 are separate events. Repeated reason codes need investigation, not automatic deduplication. Company CO003 is the fictional Birch Tools Ltd discussed in interview.
Monthly performance: full lender population, not sample-derived. Received, decided and withdrawn are period flows; open_at_period_end is stock. Decided includes cases received earlier. April starts with 25 open: 25+160−145−9=31; subsequent monthly stocks follow the same basis. Mean duration is calendar days for completed decisions only, excluding unresolved and withdrawn cases.
Do not infer funding, creditworthiness or model performance from the cases. Standard/elevated are recorded classifications, not independently validated risk scores.
